# Earth-Independent Medical Operations (EIMO) Concept of Operations

> Fuente: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20240001402/downloads/2024_IWS_EIMO_ConOps_FINAL.pdf

NASA Human Research Program, Exploration Medical Capability (ExMC) Element. Presentation at the HRP Investigators' Workshop, 13 February 2024 (Parker, Krihak, Laing, Bardina, Anderson). Extract.

## Goal

Progressive Earth-independent crew health and performance systems for Mars-class missions: **monitor and maintain crew health and performance during communication delays and in an environment that does not allow emergency evacuation or terrestrial medical assistance.**

EIMO is "the gradual transition of medical care and decision making from terrestrial to space-based assets, enabling support of astronaut health and performance and reducing overall mission risk".

## As distance from Earth increases, medical risk increases

| International Space Station (current state) | Exploration-class mission (Mars transit) |
|---|---|
| 180- to 360-day missions | 650- to more than 900-day missions |
| Strong consumables resupply | Zero consumables resupply |
| **Real-time communications** | **No real-time communications + blackouts** |
| **Emergency evacuations possible** | **No evacuations possible** |
| Limited onboard medical care (Earth-reliant) | Expanded onboard medical care (crew/vehicle-reliant) |

## Components

1. **Pre-mission planning** — onboard knowledge, skills and abilities (KSA) requirements; allocation and training of KSA by crewmember; routine onboard training and just-in-time training (JITT).
2. **Acute and emergent management decision making** — communication latency and reduced data transmission bandwidth; KSA with an onboard **Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)**.
3. **Prolonged medical decision making** — non-emergent condition management less dependent on real-time ground support; high baseline KSA and access to the onboard CDSS; mixed reality augmentation, AI and machine learning.
4. **Supplies and resource management** — no resupply; expiration dates and stressors require selective supplies.
5. **Task load management** — delays or lack of ground support mean more task demand, task switching and cognitive load (all shown to impair decision making); identify tasks that can be automated to reduce cognitive load; inventory, resource location and medical record retrieval.

The Autonomous Medical Operations (AMO) project is developing an **onboard software-based Medical Decision Support System (MDSS)** "which will enable astronauts to diagnose and treat emergent conditions in a timely manner". EIMO uses data streams from multiple sources (including the environmental control and life support system and the Crew Health & Performance Integrated Data Architecture) to assess crew health and performance and makes recommendations using AI-supported natural language processing and machine learning.

The ConOps is built from nine medical scenarios with activity diagrams; scenario 6 is **abdominal pain treatment**.
